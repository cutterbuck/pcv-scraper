Peter Cooper Village Apartment Availability



This application scrapes the Stuyvesant Town website for 2 bedroom, 2 bathroom availability in Peter Cooper Village.

StuyTown posts new availability to their website early in the morning, usually between 4-6AM. Below-market price apartments become available on rare occasions. Current and aspiring residents are known to wake up early in the morning to check for below-market listings and race to reserve these apartments. The early bird gets the cheap apartment.

This application uses Selenium and Google Chrome to scrape the Stuytown website every 15 minutes between 3AM and 7AM every morning. It renders all availability in the browser. Should a below-market listing appear, the app immediately sends a push notification to my phone. This alert wakes me so I can reserve the apartment before any of the competition.
